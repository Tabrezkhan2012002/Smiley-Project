# Smiley | Hyperlocal Product Discovery Platform

**Discover Local. Connect Directly. Shop Smarter.**

Smiley is a hyperlocal product discovery platform designed to bridge the gap between local retailers and digital consumers. It enables local businesses to establish their digital presence, showcase their product catalogs, and connect with customers within their geographical vicinity.

Unlike conventional e-commerce platforms that primarily focus on online transactions and delivery, Smiley focuses on **product discoverability at the local level**, helping customers explore products available in nearby physical stores.

---

## Overview

Local retailers often struggle to establish an online presence, while customers have limited visibility into the products offered by nearby stores.

Smiley addresses this disconnect through a centralized platform where shopkeepers can publish their product listings and customers can discover local products through an interactive, location-aware experience.

The platform aims to make local commerce more accessible, discoverable, and digitally connected.

## Core Features

- **Location-Based Discovery:** Discover nearby shops and products using geographical location.
- **Business Profiles:** Dedicated profiles for shopkeepers to establish their digital presence.
- **Product Management:** Create and manage product listings with images, names, descriptions, and pricing.
- **Interactive Product Feed:** Explore products published by local businesses.
- **User Authentication:** Secure authentication and role-based functionality for customers and shopkeepers.
- **Direct Communication:** Connect customers with shopkeepers regarding product inquiries.
- **Cloud-Based Media Storage:** Product image management through Cloudinary.
- **Geospatial Search:** Location-aware discovery using MongoDB geospatial capabilities.

## System Architecture

Smiley follows a client-server architecture:

1. **Frontend:** React.js provides the user interface and communicates with backend services through API requests.
2. **Backend:** Node.js and Express.js handle business logic, authentication, product management, and location-based operations.
3. **Database:** MongoDB stores user profiles, shop information, product details, and geographical coordinates.
4. **Media Management:** Cloudinary handles product image uploads and media delivery.

## Project Vision

The long-term vision of Smiley is to build a digital ecosystem for local commerce where physical stores can become discoverable online without requiring a complete transition to traditional e-commerce.

By connecting geographical proximity with product discovery, Smiley aims to strengthen the relationship between local businesses and their surrounding communities.
